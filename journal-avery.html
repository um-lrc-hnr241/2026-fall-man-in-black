Repetitive behavior, discipline, and detachment

When repetitive behavior changed me and how it did so: Repetitive training in soccer changed me in a multitude of ways. Repetition not only enhanced the skills I was doing day after day, but it made me more confident in what I was able to do. I feel all those years of it has created the confidence in myself even into today, even though I haven’t actually played in a while.

I feel, while both have played a role in my overall experience, discipline has been more applicable than detachment. Discipline (repetition) has built my consciousness to be and realize itself as free. Training myself to be more than what I was at birth or when I was younger.

October 7

Wittgenstein on consciousness, freedom, and self

Wittgenstein might describe consciousness as a thing that does not need one definition. It has many different characteristics and aspects that may define (in a way?) it. It is different for everybody, but there are some characteristics we can identify as parts that make up consciousness. Consciousness is more of an expression than a definition.

I think he would say something similar about freedom. Freedom is more of a mindset or expression than one that has a defining characteristic, because freedom is different for everyone and everything. There is always a new definition for it, and no single person can come up with a single definition that applies sufficiently among thought, logic, feelings, and opinions.

Wittgenstein about the self: I believe he would say that the self is like the rope, that it is defined by its many different strands together rather than as a singular being. The self is the collection of the aspects and characteristics that one may consider to make us up. One definition is not sufficient nor accurate to say.
